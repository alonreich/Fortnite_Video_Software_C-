--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 0. DECISIONS LOCKED BY THE USER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

D1. MEMES ARE A LIST, NOT ONE FILE.
    The user may place several memes at several moments in one video.

D2. A MEME IS A CUTAWAY, NOT AN OVERLAY.
    Gameplay pauses, the meme plays in full, gameplay resumes from the exact
    frame it stopped on. The finished video grows by the meme's length.

D3. THE X-AXIS TIMELINE MUST TELL THE TRUTH, EVERYWHERE.
    Explicit user requirement, wider than the meme feature: ANY operation that
    stretches or shrinks the finished video must be visible as real time on
    EVERY timeline in the app — Granular Speed Editor, Main App opening screen,
    Music Wizard, Voice-Over screens. A 1.5-second freeze must add 1.5 seconds
    to every ruler. Slow motion must lengthen them. A meme must lengthen them.

D4. MUSIC DURING MEMES — ONE ANSWER FOR ALL MEMES (Option A).
    A single global choice, reused for every meme in the video. No per-meme
    override. The existing KeepMusicDuringMeme flag already models exactly this
    and is reused unchanged.

D5. VOICE-OVER BEHAVIOUR AROUND AN INSERTED MEME — TWO RULES, BY POSITION.
    (a) Meme lands BEFORE a take  -> the take slides later, intact.
        Take at 3s-5s, 2s meme prepended -> take now plays 5s-7s.
    (b) Meme lands INSIDE a take  -> the take splits in two and resumes.
        Take at 3s-5s, 2s meme placed at 4s -> take plays 3s-4s, meme plays,
        then the take CONTINUES FROM WHERE IT STOPPED at 6s-7s.
    Nothing is lost, nothing is repeated, nothing overlaps.

D6. NO CAP ON THE NUMBER OF MEMES PER VIDEO.

D7. TWO MEMES MAY NOT SHARE ONE INSERTION POINT.
    The second placement is blocked and the user is told a meme already sits
    there. Placement must therefore be validated before it is accepted.

D8. A MEME NEVER INTERRUPTS A FREEZE OR A SPEED SEGMENT.
    If the chosen point falls inside a freeze or a slow-motion segment, the
    meme waits until that segment has finished, then plays. It is never
    sandwiched into the middle of one. Consequence: the stored insertion point
    must be SNAPPED FORWARD to the end of any segment it lands inside, and the
    UI marker must be drawn at the snapped position so what the user sees is
    where the meme will actually land.

D9. THE TIMELINE FIX SHIPS FIRST, ON ITS OWN.
    Phases 1 and 2 are released and tested as a self-contained change before
    any meme work begins. Rationale: they fix a defect that exists today, and
    isolating the preview-clock change from brand-new meme code means a
    regression can be attributed to one or the other, not both.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. THE CORE PROBLEM, IN PLAIN ENGLISH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Today the app assumes every second of the finished video came from a second
    of the source video. Speed changes bend that relationship. Freezes break it.
    A meme destroys it — during a meme the finished video is advancing while the
    source video is not moving at all.

    So the app needs to stop asking "how long is the source video?" and start
    asking "how long is the finished video?" — and it needs ONE place that knows
    the answer, instead of two half-answers in two projects.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. TARGET ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    ONE shared timeline model, in FortniteVideoSoftware.Core.Media, owning four
    responsibilities that are today scattered or missing:

      1. source -> output            (exists: GranularSpeedBuilder:23)
      2. output -> source            (exists: MusicWizardWindow:2517 — move it)
      3. total output duration       (exists: MusicWizardWindow:2483 — move it)
      4. insertions (memes, stills)  (NEW)

    Every ruler in the app then asks this one object two questions: how long is
    the finished video, and where is the playhead in finished-video time.

    Freezes, slow motion, memes and the thumbnail still all lengthen every
    timeline automatically, because exactly one place decides.

    3.1 REVERSE MAPPING IS DELIBERATELY LOSSY, AND THAT IS CORRECT.
        During a freeze or a meme, many output moments map to ONE source moment.
        The model returns that single frozen source instant, and additionally
        reports WHICH insertion the playhead is inside (or none). Callers that
        only need a seek target ignore the extra information; the UI uses it to
        show the meme marker as active.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. THE HONEST LIMITATION — READ BEFORE APPROVING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    MPV PLAYS THE SOURCE FILE. IT CANNOT PLAY THE FINISHED TIMELINE.

    Making the rulers show output time means the ruler stops being "mpv's own
    clock" and becomes an app-driven clock backed by mpv. You scrub in output
    time; the app converts and seeks mpv to the matching source frame. During a
    freeze or a meme the clock advances while mpv sits still on one frame.

    Consequences to accept up front:
      - The preview will NOT play the meme's own video or audio inline. The
        timeline shows a marker and the clock runs; mpv holds the last frame.
      - Playback position now comes from an app-side clock during insertions,
        not from mpv's reported time. This is the riskiest change in the plan —
        more so than the export graph work, which is largely already built.

    This is a real behaviour change to the preview and it is not optional if
    D3 is to be honoured.

 MEME_04 — INSERTIONS IN THE MODEL. DONE 2026-08-15. Proven, not compiled.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`OutputTimeline` now accepts `IReadOnlyList<Insertion>`. A meme occupies output time and
consumes NO source time, exactly like a freeze, so nothing after it is lost - it is pushed
later. New API: `Insertion(AtSourceSec, DurationSec, Id)`, `Chunk.IsInsertion`,
`Chunk.HoldsSource`, `Chunk.FromSegment`, `InsertionAt(outputSec)`,
`SnapInsertionPoint(sourceSec)` (D8), `HasInsertionAtSource(snapped)` (D7).

TWO REAL DEFECTS WERE CAUGHT BY THE VERIFICATION AND FIXED - RECORDED SO THEY ARE NOT REDONE:

  B1. THE CONTAINING FOOTAGE CHUNK MUST BE SPLIT AT THE INSERTION POINT. Without the split a
      meme 'at 30s' of a 60s clip with no segments was APPENDED AFTER the whole 0-60 chunk, so
      it played at the END and the reverse mapping jumped backwards from 60s to 30s. Caught by
      the monotonicity check, which is why that check must stay in any future test.

  B2. DO NOT ADD A 'STEP PAST THE FREEZE' PASS. The index search already skips holding chunks,
      so it lands on the first MOVING footage at or after the instant - which is already the
      slot after any freeze there. An extra increment jumps the meme past the resumed footage
      too and sends it to the end of the clip.

BOUNDARY SEMANTICS, NOT A BUG: at the exact instant a hold BEGINS, source->output already
counts the whole hold (`if (target >= start) mapped += hold`). That single sample is ambiguous
by design and is ORIGINAL SHIPPED BEHAVIOUR preserved byte-for-byte by TIME_01. Any test must
exclude the hold-start instant or it will report a false failure equal to the hold length.

VERIFIED across 9 scenarios (1/3/10 memes, prepend, append, meme+freeze on the same instant,
meme+slow-motion, memes+freeze+slow-motion, trimmed clip): finished length equals base plus the
sum of the meme durations EXACTLY; each meme occupies exactly its own span; the inverse is exact
to 0.000000000s wherever the picture moves; the playhead never runs backwards.

  durations into the cut time, and each meme after the first would drift later by the sum of the

  Cut in ASCENDING order and keep the pairs interleaved [v][a][v][a] — concat needs them adjacent.

WHAT CHANGED — all of it inside src/FortniteVideoSoftware.Core/Media/ProcessWorker.cs.

  1. NEW private type `ResolvedMeme` + helpers `SanitizeMemeLabel` and `CutSec`.
     One meme, fully resolved: probed duration, image/silent flags, its own measured
     loudness gain (MEME_01), its FFmpeg input index, its filter labels [<id>_v]/[<id>_a],
     its cut time in the rendered stream and its slot in the concat.

  2. THE SINGLE-MEME SCALARS ARE GONE. `memeDuration`, `memeIsImage`, `memeHasAudio`,
     `memeLoudnessGainDb`, `memeLoudnessMeasured`, `memeInputIndex` no longer exist. They
     are now per-entry state on `ResolvedMeme`; only `memeTotalDuration` (the sum) survives
     as a scalar, and it is what every budget/duration/`-t` calculation now uses.
     `memeWidth`/`memeHeight` were probed and never read by anything — the ffprobe call
     that produced them was deleted. That is one fewer probe per export and changes no output.

  3. THE PROBE IS A LOOP. `MemePlacements` wins when non-empty; when empty the legacy
     MemeFile + MemeAtStart pair is projected into ONE placement (id kept as "meme", so the
     emitted labels stay [meme_v]/[meme_a]). Missing files are logged and skipped instead of
     killing the export. DURATION IS RE-PROBED rather than trusted from the placement — the
     trims, the bitrate budget and the `-t` ceiling all depend on it.

  4. ONE `-i` PER MEME, in the same order the input indices were handed out. Image memes keep
     `-loop 1 -framerate <fps> -t <dur>` and NO hwaccel; video memes keep their own decodeFlags
     (G04). The voice-over base index now skips `memes.Count` inputs, not one.

  5. THE CONCAT IS GENERALISED. Cut times are computed, the rendered stream is split into
     pieces at every interior cut, and memes are interleaved as [v][a] pairs.

HOW THE CUT TIME IS COMPUTED — THE PART THAT WAS FLAGGED AS EASIEST TO GET WRONG:

    double bodyOutSec = granularTimeMapper != null
        ? granularTimeMapper(StartTimeMs/1000.0 + m.AtSourceSecRelative)
        : (StartTimeMs/1000.0 + m.AtSourceSecRelative - actualExtractStartMs/1000.0) / SpeedFactor;
    cut = clamp(introDurationSec + bodyOutSec, introDurationSec, renderDurationSec);
    cut = round(cut * fps) / fps;

  * `granularTimeMapper` IS `OutputTimeline.SourceToOutput` built WITHOUT insertions
    (GranularSpeedBuilder.Build:342). That is exactly the pre-meme clock the brief demanded.
    It is also the SAME mapper the voice-over start positions use, so meme cuts and voice-over
    starts cannot disagree.
  * `+ introDurationSec` IS NOT OPTIONAL and the cold-start brief omitted it. `[v_render_out]`
    already carries the frozen thumbnail head, so a cut computed in the granular clock alone
    would land introDurationSec too early — and a meme "at 0" would land in FRONT of the
    thumbnail, which is precisely the bug MEME_02 exists to prevent. The lower clamp is
    introDurationSec for the same reason.
  * A meme at (or before) AtSourceSecRelative == 0 has its body offset forced to 0. Without
    that the lead-in fade pad (footage from BEFORE the trim-in point) makes the mapper report
    ~0.5-1.0s and the meme gets dropped in AFTER the fade-in instead of leading it.

NEW: CUTS ARE SNAPPED TO THE FRAME GRID — round(t*fps)/fps.
  `trim` can only cut on a frame boundary; `atrim` is sample-accurate. An unsnapped cut makes
  the video piece and the audio piece different lengths by up to half a frame EACH, and N seams
  accumulate that error. At 60fps/48kHz one frame is exactly 800 samples, so a snapped cut is
  exact on both sides. This is a strict improvement over the shipped 3-way split. DO NOT REMOVE.

SLOTS AND THE CONCAT ORDER:
  cuts    = interior cut times, ascending, distinct.
  pieces  = cuts.Count + 1 trims off [v_render_out] / the final audio label.
  slot 0  = ahead of piece 0 (only reachable when there is NO thumbnail head to protect)
  slot i  = between piece i-1 and piece i
  slot N  = after everything (a meme at the end — no cut needed)
  concat order = [memes in slot 0][piece 0][memes in slot 1][piece 1]...[memes in last slot]
  Every entry contributes its [v] and its [a] ADJACENTLY. concat reads positionally; one
  transposed pair welds the wrong audio to the wrong picture.

  MinPieceSec = 0.05. Two cuts closer than that would leave a piece with no frames in it and
  concat fails to configure, so the later cut is merged onto the earlier one and the memes
  stack at the same seam — which is the right answer anyway, they are at the same moment.
  The thumbnail head is exempt: it is always the FIRST cut, so there is nothing to merge onto.

  split / asplit are EXPLICIT and always will be. Handing one label to several filters lets
  FFmpeg improvise the duplication, and this codebase has already lost a full second of video
  to exactly that (the implicit-split A/V desync in the changelog).

LEGACY EQUIVALENCE — PROVEN BY SIMULATING THE EMITTER, all four shipped forms reproduced
exactly (label names differ, e.g. v_ro_a -> v_cut0_src, v_thumb_head -> v_cut0, v_body ->
v_cut1; the emitted graph is otherwise identical and the output bytes cannot change):

  meme at END, intro present or not
      [v_render_out]{a}[meme_v][meme_a]concat=n=2:v=1:a=1[v_final][a_final_before_music]
  meme at START, no intro
      [meme_v][meme_a][v_render_out]{a}concat=n=2:v=1:a=1[v_final][a_final_before_music]
  meme at START, intro 0.1s
      [v_render_out]split=2[v_cut0_src][v_cut1_src]
      {a}asplit=2[a_cut0_src][a_cut1_src]
      [v_cut0_src]trim=start=0:end=0.100000,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS[v_cut0]
      [a_cut0_src]atrim=start=0:end=0.100000,asetpts=PTS-STARTPTS[a_cut0]
      [v_cut1_src]trim=start=0.100000,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS[v_cut1]
      [a_cut1_src]atrim=start=0.100000,asetpts=PTS-STARTPTS[a_cut1]
      [v_cut0][a_cut0][meme_v][meme_a][v_cut1][a_cut1]concat=n=3:v=1:a=1[v_final][a_final_before_music]

  Multi-meme, verified by the same simulation: 3 memes at 0/30s/end with a 0.1s intro produce
  3 pieces and concat=n=6; 10 mid-video memes produce 11 pieces and concat=n=21 (= 2N+1, the
  figure the brief predicted); two memes on the same instant stack at one seam without
  producing an empty piece.

DUCKED/DEFERRED, ON PURPOSE:
  * The `-t` ceiling is renderDurationSec + sum(meme durations). Each meme's frame count is
    round(dur*fps), so the true length can exceed the ceiling by up to half a frame PER MEME.
    Inherited from the single-meme code; matters only at very high meme counts.
  * The progress cost model still lumps all meme time into one span at the end. It is an
    estimate feeding a monotonic bar; placing the spans truthfully buys nothing.

VOICE-OVER PLACEMENT — FIXED HERE, NOT DEFERRED TO VO_01.
  MEME_05 is what makes mid-video memes reachable, so MEME_05 carries the fallout.

  NORMAL PATH (KeepMusicDuringMeme OFF). Nothing needed, and D5 falls out for free. The voice
  is mixed into the body audio BEFORE the intro is prepended and BEFORE the memes are spliced
  in, so the atrim that cuts the rendered audio at a meme seam cuts the voice with it: a take
  straddling a meme SPLITS AND RESUMES (D5b) and a take after a meme SLIDES LATER INTACT (D5a).
  The pre-meme body clock is exactly the right clock there.

  KEEP-MUSIC PATH (KeepMusicDuringMeme ON) WAS BROKEN. There the voice is mixed AFTER the
  concat, onto a stream whose t=0 is the start of the thumbnail intro and which already contains
  every meme — but the `adelay` was still computed in the body clock. Every take was therefore
  early TWICE: by introDurationSec, and by the total length of every meme playing before it.
  Invisible historically (memes only ever sat at the very end); seconds out of place the moment
  a meme lands mid-video. Now:

      double voDelaySec = voStartOutSec;                       // body clock
      if (mixMusicAfterMeme)
          voDelaySec += introDurationSec + MemeTimeInsertedBefore(introDurationSec + voStartOutSec);

  `MemeTimeInsertedBefore(t)` sums the durations of every meme whose cut time is at or before t.
  The boundary is INCLUSIVE on purpose: a meme sitting exactly on that instant plays before it,
  because the rendered stream is cut there and resumes after the meme.

  ⚠️ THE DUCKING PULSE DELIBERATELY KEEPS THE BODY CLOCK. It acts on `aPreparedPad`, which is
  the pre-intro, pre-meme audio. Do not "fix" it to match the delay above — that would move the
  duck away from the voice it is meant to duck under.

  ⚠️ THIS IS WHY THE CUT TIMES ARE COMPUTED BEFORE THE VOICE-OVER BLOCK and not down at the
  concat where they are consumed. `memeCuts` / `memePieceCount` / `MemeTimeInsertedBefore` are
  built right after `renderDurationSec`. Moving them back down reintroduces the defect.

  Worked example — intro 0.1s, 60s body, one 4s meme at body-time 30s, take at body-time 40s:
      final layout   [0-0.1 intro][0.1-30.1 body 0-30][30.1-34.1 meme][34.1-64.1 body 30-60]
      take belongs at 34.1 + (40-30) = 44.1s
      new delay      40 + 0.1 + 4 = 44.1s   ✓      old delay 40s — 4.1s early.

FOUR MORE PLACES WERE MEASURING IN THE PRE-MEME CLOCK. ALL FIXED HERE.
  A full audit was run over the repo after the voice-over fix, because that defect was clearly a
  CLASS of defect, not a one-off: anything positioned in the pre-meme clock but consumed against
  the finished video is wrong the moment a meme lands mid-video.

  (a) THE THUMBNAIL GRAB (ProcessWorker, the `ThumbnailPosMs > 0` block). It seeks the FINISHED
      file, but every term of `extractTargetSec` was pre-meme, so it landed early by the meme time
      in front of the chosen moment — far enough, with a mid-video meme, to grab a frame OF THE
      MEME instead of the gameplay the user picked. Now shifted by
      `MemeTimeInsertedBefore(..., landsAfter: true)`.
      (Note: nothing in the app currently reads that .jpg — it is produced, logged and discarded
      into tempJobDir. Fixed anyway; the moment it is surfaced it must already be right.)

  (b) MUSIC START DELAY ON THE KEEP-MUSIC PATH. `MusicTrack.Duration` was extended by the meme
      total, but `TimelineStartDelay` was left in the body clock, so a track starting partway in
      came in early by every meme before it — the same double-early shape as the voice-over.
      Now shifted with `landsAfter: false`; see the boundary rule below.
      `Duration` is deliberately left long: AudioFilterChain sequences consecutive tracks with
      `accumProjectSec += Duration` AND adds TimelineStartDelay on top, so trimming Duration would
      drag every LATER track's start with it. Overshooting the end is bounded by the `-t` ceiling
      and the tail fade; undershooting would leave silence.

  (c) THE CLOSING FADE PAD. `padEndHumanSec = 0` fired whenever ANY meme existed, on the reasoning
      "the gameplay is no longer last". That stopped being true: with every meme inside the clip
      the gameplay IS last, and the export lost its closing fade for no reason. Now dropped only
      when a meme actually ends the video.
      ⚠️ THIS DELIBERATELY CHANGES LEGACY BEHAVIOUR FOR MemeAtStart=true. CONFIRMED BY THE OWNER
      2026-08-16 — keep the fix, the old behaviour was a bug. That case zeroed the pad
      even though the meme played FIRST and the gameplay still ended the video — always wrong, and
      it never mattered while "meme" and "meme at the end" were nearly synonymous. Such an export
      now regains its closing fade (and up to 1s more source footage past MARK END). This is the
      ONE respect in which a legacy payload no longer exports byte-identically. Revert by making
      the test unconditional again if the old look is wanted.

  (d) THE MUSIC TAIL FADE was sized by `memes[^1].DurationSec` — "whatever is last on screen". But
      `memes` is sorted by CUT POSITION, so with every meme mid-video that is a clip playing in the
      middle, and the fade started in the wrong place over footage that was not ending. Now it is
      sized by the last meme only when that meme actually occupies the trailing slot.

THE BOUNDARY RULE — `MemeTimeInsertedBefore(t, landsAfter)`:
  What happens to something sitting EXACTLY on a meme's instant is not one answer, it is two.
    landsAfter: true  — a VOICE-OVER take and the THUMBNAIL frame are locked to a moment of
                        GAMEPLAY, and that gameplay resumes AFTER the meme. They move with it.
    landsAfter: false — MUSIC on the KeepMusicDuringMeme path is explicitly meant to play THROUGH
                        memes, so a cue on that instant starts WITH the meme.
  ⚠️ Getting this backwards for music is not a rounding error: a track with start delay 0 would
  begin AFTER a leading meme — silence over the very meme the flag exists to keep music under.

STILL OPEN — DELIBERATELY, WITH REASONS:
  * THE INTRO OFFSET ON THE KEEP-MUSIC PATH. Music and voice-over are laid over a stream whose t=0
    is the thumbnail intro, so both are also introDurationSec early there (one frame at the default
    0.1s). The voice-over fix above corrects it; the MUSIC one does not, because moving the music
    start also moves the tail fade the Music Wizard's markers were tuned against. Pre-existing,
    unrelated to memes, and a Music Wizard decision. Take it with the Wizard in view.
  * (CLOSED 2026-08-16, owner's decision.) THE MID-VIDEO MEME FADE-OUT is gone; a mid-video meme
    now HARD-CUTS ON BOTH SIDES, the classic cutaway. The fade is kept exactly where something
    exists to fade into:
        TRAILING meme -> the video ends; fading to black IS the ending.          KEEP
        LEADING meme  -> the gameplay behind it opens with its own `fade=t=in`
                         over the lead-in pad, so the two form one transition.   KEEP
        INTERIOR meme -> gameplay resumes mid-render with no fade-in, so a fade
                         to black is followed by a hard cut OUT of black.        SKIP
    Because both legacy placements are either trailing or leading, both shipped forms keep their
    fade unchanged — the new rule only ever fires on a placement the old code could not express.
  * THE `-t` CEILING can be short by up to half a frame PER MEME, because each meme's frame count
    is round(dur*fps) while the ceiling sums the raw probed durations. Inherited from the
    single-meme code; only visible at high meme counts.
  * THE PROGRESS COST MODEL still lumps all meme time into one span at the end. It is an estimate
    feeding a monotonic bar.

  * GranularSpeedEditorWindow `OutTimeline()` likewise omits `insertions`, so `OutDurationSec()`,

  * MainWindow `CalculateEffectiveDurationMs` is a FOURTH hand-written copy of the output-time

  * MergerWorker has no meme handling at all — audited, nothing to do.
  * The export does NOT re-validate D7 (two memes on one point) or D8 (a meme inside a speed
